Services

Personal help for real kitchens

Our free guides cover the fundamentals. When you'd like advice shaped around your own cooking, schedule or organization, these services are here to help.

For individuals & couples

Private online cooking lessons

One-to-one lessons over video, cooked in your own kitchen with your own equipment. Before we begin, we'll ask what you'd like to learn — knife skills, weeknight dinners, bread, confident seasoning — and plan a lesson around it.

  • Shopping list sent ahead of each lesson
  • Guidance adapted to your equipment and skill level
  • Written recap with the key techniques afterwards
Enquire about lessons

For busy households

Meal-planning sessions

A relaxed session to build a realistic weekly plan around your schedule, budget and tastes. We focus on batch-friendly bases, smart use of leftovers and seasonal produce so you spend less time deciding and more time eating well.

  • A flexible one-week plan and consolidated shopping list
  • Prep-ahead steps for busy evenings
  • Ideas for using up what's already in your pantry
Enquire about meal planning

For teams, schools & community groups

Group cooking workshops

Friendly, practical workshops for groups — online or in person in the New York area. Popular themes include seasonal cooking, bread basics, family baking and spices of the world. Each session is hands-on, inclusive and tailored to your group.

  • Themes agreed in advance with the organizer
  • Ingredient lists and recipe cards for every participant
  • Options for common dietary requirements
Enquire about workshops

For food businesses & publishers

Recipe development & testing

Clear, reliable recipes developed and tested in home kitchens for small food brands, cafés and publishers. We write in plain language, measure in both volume and weight, and test until results are consistent.

  • Original recipes to your brief
  • Independent testing of existing recipes
  • US and metric measurements
Enquire about recipe work

For brands & websites

Food writing & how-to guides

Helpful, accurate cooking content — technique explainers, ingredient guides and seasonal features — written for real cooks and checked against recognized food-safety guidance.

  • Practical, reader-first writing
  • Fact-checked temperatures and storage advice
  • Clear structure that's easy to scan
