Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Nutmeg Spruce (“we”, “us”), of 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, collects and uses personal information when you visit nutmegspruce.com, use our kitchen tools, send us a message, enquire about a service or subscribe to our newsletter. We collect as little as possible, use it only for clear purposes and never sell it. Questions are welcome at hello@nutmegspruce.com or +1-888-777-5845.

2. Information we collect

  • Messages and enquiries: your name, email address, the topic or service you choose, preferred timing and anything you write to us.
  • Newsletter: your email address and the date you subscribed.
  • Service bookings: if you book a lesson or workshop, the details needed to schedule and deliver it, and confirmation that payment was made. Payments are handled by our payment provider; we never see full card details.
  • Website usage: pages visited, device and browser type and approximate location, collected through cookies only if you allow them.

Our kitchen tools — the seasonal produce explorer, measurement converter and spice pairing finder — run entirely in your browser. We don't record what you enter into them.

3. How we use it

We use your information to reply to messages, prepare proposals and deliver services, send the newsletter you asked for, keep simple business records, and — with your consent — understand which pages are useful so we can improve them. Every newsletter includes a one-click unsubscribe link.

4. Legal grounds

Where privacy laws require it, we rely on your consent (newsletter, optional cookies), the need to respond to your request or deliver a service you've asked for, and our legitimate interest in running a secure, useful website. You can withdraw consent at any time.

5. Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us run Nutmeg Spruce — such as our website host, email provider, newsletter platform, video-call platform for lessons and payment processor — and only as needed for their service. We may disclose information where the law requires it. We do not sell or rent personal information.

6. Google Analytics and advertising

With your consent, we use Google Analytics and may show ads served by Google. We use Google Consent Mode, so analytics and advertising cookies are not set until you choose “Accept all” in our cookie banner. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites; you can manage ad personalization in your Google account settings. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

7. How long we keep it

Messages and enquiries are kept for up to two years. Service records and invoices are kept for seven years for tax purposes. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe. After that, information is deleted or anonymized.

8. Security

Our website uses HTTPS encryption, access to personal information is limited, and we choose reputable service providers. No online service is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information and will notify you where the law requires if a breach occurs.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, receive a copy of it, object to certain uses and opt out of targeted advertising. California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA. To make a request, email hello@nutmegspruce.com; we'll respond within the time allowed by law.

10. Children

Our website is intended for a general adult audience. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Children's workshops are always arranged with and through a parent, guardian or organizer.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The current version is always on this page, with its date at the top.
