Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files websites save on your device, and local storage is a similar browser feature. They let a site remember information between pages or visits — for example, your cookie choice. This policy explains what nutmegspruce.com stores, why, and how you can change your mind at any time.

4. Our kitchen tools

The converter, produce explorer and spice finder work entirely in your browser and don't store anything about what you enter.

5. Consent Mode

We use Google Consent Mode. Until you select “Accept all”, analytics and advertising storage stay set to “denied”, and Google receives only limited, cookieless signals. You can learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/cookies.

6. Images

Photographs are loaded from the Pexels image service. As with any image on the web, loading one shares basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type with that service.

7. Managing cookies

You can view and delete cookies and site data in your browser's privacy settings, or block them altogether. Blocking cookies won't stop our guides or tools from working.
