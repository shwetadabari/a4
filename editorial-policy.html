Editorial Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Our purpose

Nutmeg Spruce exists to help home cooks cook with more confidence. Every page we publish should leave a reader better able to make a good meal safely. This Editorial Policy explains how we research, test, write and update our content, so you know what you can expect from us.

2. Research and sources

Food-safety information — including cooking temperatures and storage guidance — is based on recognized public sources such as the USDA and FoodSafety.gov. Where we rely on a specific figure, we link to or name the source. For techniques and ingredient advice, we draw on established cooking practice and our own kitchen testing.

3. Testing in real kitchens

We try our techniques and tips in ordinary home kitchens with everyday equipment, not just professional ones. Measurement conversions are based on widely published reference weights for each ingredient. If a method only works with specialist equipment, we say so.

4. How we write

  • Plain, friendly language, with any technical terms explained.
  • Honest limitations — we note when results vary by region, equipment or ingredient.
  • No exaggerated health or nutrition claims; we write about cooking, not medicine.
  • Original writing: we do not copy text from other publications.

5. Independence

Our guidance is not influenced by advertisers. If the site shows ads, they are clearly separate from our content and don't affect what we recommend. If we ever work with a brand on a service such as recipe development, that work is kept separate from our independent guides, and any sponsored content would be clearly labeled.

6. Reviews and updates

We review pages regularly and whenever relevant public guidance changes. Pages that contain food-safety information are checked against current official sources at least once a year.

7. Corrections

If you notice an error, please email hello@nutmegspruce.com with the page and the issue. We review every request, correct genuine errors promptly, and make meaningful corrections clear on the page.

8. Images

Photographs are licensed from Pexels and chosen to illustrate the topic of each section. They are illustrative and may not show the exact method or ingredients described.
