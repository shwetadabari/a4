Disclaimer

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. General information only

The content on nutmegspruce.com — including cooking techniques, pantry advice, seasonal produce information, spice pairings, measurement conversions and food-safety tips — is general information for home cooks. We work hard to make it accurate and up to date, but cooking results depend on your ingredients, equipment and methods, and we can't guarantee any particular outcome.

2. Food safety

Our safe-temperature table reflects widely used public guidance from sources such as the USDA and FoodSafety.gov at the time of writing. Guidance can change, so please check official sources for the latest recommendations. Always use a reliable food thermometer, practice good hygiene and follow safe storage times. People who are pregnant, older adults, young children and anyone with a weakened immune system should take extra care and follow official guidance for higher-risk groups.

3. Allergies and dietary needs

Our content does not account for every allergy or intolerance. Always read ingredient labels, check for cross-contamination, and make substitutions suitable for your needs. If you or anyone you cook for has a food allergy, follow the advice of a medical professional.

4. Not medical or nutrition advice

We write about cooking, not medicine or nutrition therapy. Nothing on this website is intended to diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, or to replace advice from a doctor or registered dietitian.

5. Kitchen tools and conversions

Our measurement converter uses typical weights for common ingredients. Actual weights vary with brand, moisture and how ingredients are measured, so results are close guides rather than exact values. Seasonal produce information is a general guide for the United States and varies by region and year.

6. Equipment and kitchen safety

Knives, hot pans, ovens and appliances can cause injury. Use equipment according to its manufacturer's instructions, keep children supervised, and never leave cooking unattended.

7. External links and images

Links to other websites are provided for convenience; we don't control their content. Photographs are licensed from Pexels and are used to illustrate topics; they may not show the exact method described.

8. Contact

If you spot something that looks inaccurate, please tell us at hello@nutmegspruce.com. We review every correction request, as described in our Editorial Policy.
